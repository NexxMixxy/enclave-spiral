Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide character proxying and session management, Enclave Spiral collects and stores:

  • GamerTags & Passwords: Account usernames and SHA-256 password hashes.
  • Discord IDs: Linked numeric Discord user IDs when interacting with bot commands.
  • Character Profiles: Avatar names, cropped avatar images stored securely in our private Google Drive folder, and slot status settings.
  • Draft Content: Active post drafts temporarily saved to prevent text loss during character limit checks.

2. How Data Is Stored

All user records, character configurations, and draft states are securely stored within our private Google Spreadsheet database and Google Drive storage linked via Google Apps Script. Data is never shared with third-party advertisers or external services.

3. Data Deletion & Requests

Users may request complete deletion of their account, character profiles, and uploaded avatar images at any time by contacting server administrators or reaching out via the Enclave Spiral administration channels.
